# 🎓 EduMind AI

### AI-Powered Intelligent Learning & Career Assistant for Engineering Students

> **"Don't just learn. Let AI understand how you learn."**

EduMind AI is an advanced AI-powered educational platform designed specifically for **Engineering College students**.

Unlike a traditional chatbot that simply answers questions, EduMind AI creates a personalized **Student Intelligence Profile** and uses academic performance, learning behavior, mistakes, skills, projects, and career goals to provide intelligent and adaptive assistance.

---

## 🚀 Why EduMind AI?

Engineering students often use multiple disconnected platforms for:

- 📚 Learning subjects
- 📝 Exam preparation
- 💻 Coding practice
- 🧪 Laboratory preparation
- 🎤 Viva preparation
- 🚀 Project development
- 💼 Placement preparation
- 📄 Resume improvement

EduMind AI brings these capabilities together into a **single intelligent platform**.
